Speaking Topics

Ahmet speaks from decades of lived experience — not theory. Each keynote and workshop draws on real decisions made in real hotels, across multiple countries and market conditions.

Signature Keynote

The Leadership Standard: What 30 Years in Hotel Management Taught Me

A personal, honest account of what separates exceptional leaders from competent managers — told through the lens of global hospitality. Suitable for hospitality conferences, business leadership events, and executive retreats. Anchors to the cornerstone essay.

Audience: Hotel GMs, senior operators, leadership teams, business school audiences Format: Keynote (45–75 min) · Half-day workshop with exercises
Culture & Teams

Culture Is the Product: Building High-Performance Hospitality Teams

How the best-performing hotel teams are built — not through process alone, but through deliberate culture. Covers hiring for character, real-time feedback, dignity-centred leadership, and creating teams that perform under pressure without being managed into it.

Audience: HR leaders, department heads, multi-property operators Format: Half-day or full-day workshop · Keynote version available
Guest Experience

The Guest Experience Equation: Standards, Emotion, and Trust at Speed

Hospitality has always understood something every industry is now trying to learn: people remember how they were made to feel, not what they were sold. This session translates hotel-grade guest experience thinking into any service environment.

Audience: Customer experience leaders, retail and service executives, frontline management teams Format: Keynote (45–60 min) · Interactive workshop
GM Masterclass

GM Operations Masterclass: Revenue, People, and the Property You Are Responsible For

A practical, senior-level deep dive into the interconnected systems of hotel general management — revenue, guest satisfaction, team development, owner relations, and operational discipline. Based on Ahmet's Cornell GMP curriculum and 30+ years of multi-brand experience.

Audience: Hotel GMs and Deputy GMs, regional leadership, ownership groups Format: Full-day training · Half-day intensive · Multi-session programme
AI & Leadership

Hospitality Leadership in the Age of AI and Distraction

Drawing on Ahmet's book series The Missing Pieces of Life Puzzle, this session addresses the leadership challenge of our time: how to remain present, decisive, and human in organisations increasingly shaped by automation and information overload.

Audience: Executive teams, hospitality educators, leadership conferences Format: Keynote (45–60 min) · Panel contribution

Strategic Hotel Auditing

Pre-Opening Advisory

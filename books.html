Wellness · Body Intelligence

Breath

James Nestor · Popular Science

How we breathe shapes our sleep, energy, stress, endurance, and overall health — and most of us have been doing it wrong. Nestor blends science, history, and personal experiment into a genuinely eye-opening read.

Key Lessons

  • 01 Breathe through the nose whenever possible — it filters, warms, and pressurizes air optimally.
  • 02 Slow, deliberate breathing calms the nervous system far more than most people realize.
  • 03 Sleep quality and breathing quality are inseparably linked.
  • 04 Overbreathing is a silent stressor — less can genuinely be more.

Popular science — treat it as thought-provoking and practical, not a substitute for medical advice. Some claims are broader than the research fully proves.
